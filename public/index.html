How much does a wedding website cost?
Today’s launch offer is ₹999 for the first 10 customers (regularly ₹3,999). You pay once — updates, edits and the public link are included, with no recurring charges.
How fast will my website be ready?
Instantly. As soon as you finish personalising your template and publish, you get your own public link right away. You can keep editing anytime after that.
Can I change the template after publishing?
Yes — you can preview and switch designs any time. Your details are kept; the page is rebuilt in the new style.

Names, parents, invitation text, your story, hashtag, up to six wedding events (with venues, map pins and timings), a countdown, photos, gallery, and the WhatsApp number guests use for RSVP. Everything reflects live as you type.
Do my guests need an app to see it?
No. The invite opens in any phone or computer browser — it looks beautiful on all of them.
